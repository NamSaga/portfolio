**LIWA** is a science-fiction story about the last human, transferred into a virtual paradise ruled by an all-powerful AI goddess.

As the storyboarder, my main role is to bridge the author's narrative vision and the visuals of the artist in charge of the final rendering.

This project helped me strengthen my artistic skills. Turning a script into images is essential, in comics as much as in video game development, to bring out the original stories of a project.
